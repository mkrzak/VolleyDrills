Setting · 20 min · setters, then live hitters

Release and set to zones

Same body shape, any target: 4, quick, back set or pipe.

  1. Setter starts in right-back, releases on the coach's slap.
  2. Coach passes to the target and calls a zone.
  3. Setter delivers to that hoop; 10 balls, then swap.

Score in or within 1 m of the hoop 1 · clean quick or pipe +1.

  • Go on the slap
  • Stop, then square to 4
  • Hands don't tell the block

Next hitters swing at every set.

Hitting · 20 min · setter + hitter line

Approach and hit to targets

Strong approach; line and cross on purpose.

  1. Hitters queue outside 4, ~4 m off the net.
  2. Coach tosses, setter sets high to 4.
  3. Coach calls “line” or “cross”; hit that zone.

Score called zone 1 · target 2 · 10 swings each.

  • L–R–L, last two fast
  • Elbow high, contact in front
  • Turn body or wrist to aim

Next add one blocker; hitter picks the open shot.

Blocking · 15 min · front row + coach on box

Read, move, close the block

Middle reads and closes beside the outside blocker.

  1. Coach on a box attacks from either side.
  2. Right-side blocker sets the edge.
  3. Middle closes with step–cross–step; both block.

Score touch 1 · stuff 2 · ball through a gap −1.

  • Setter → ball → hitter
  • No gap in the seam
  • Press hands over the net

Next mix in tips; back row covers.

Passing · 15–20 min · 3 passers + server

Three-person serve receive

Consistent passes to target under serve pressure.

  1. Passers in 5, 6 and 1; serve from the far side.
  2. Pass to the target between 2 and 3.
  3. Rate each pass 0–3; rotate after 10.

Score average 2.0+ over 10 serves (3 = all options, 0 = ace).

  • Feet first, then platform
  • Angle to target, no swing
  • Read the server's toss

Next serve the seams and short zones.

Out of system · 15 min · 6 players

Setter digs, someone else sets

Turn a scramble into a real attack.

  1. Coach on a box hits at the setter in right-back.
  2. Setter digs high to mid-court, calls “Help!”.
  3. Right-front sets high to 4; hitter attacks.

Score wash — 2 attacks in a row wins a point, first to 5.

  • Dig high, to the middle
  • Square up or bump set
  • Hitter waits wide

Next coach points late at who takes the second ball.

Free ball · 15 min · 6 players

Free ball transition

Easy ball over → fast in-system attack.

  1. Front row at the net, setter in right-back.
  2. Coach tosses an easy ball: first player calls “Free!”.
  3. Front row drops to 3 m, setter releases, pass to target, attack.

Score in-system attack 1 · middle/combo kill 2 · missed call −1.

  • Off the net before it crosses
  • High pass to target
  • Hitters call sets early

Next a second free ball straight after the attack.

Communication · 10–15 min · 2–3 passers + server

Call the seam

Early calls so seam balls never drop.

  1. Two passers ~3 m apart; server aims at the seam.
  2. “Mine!” before the ball crosses the net; partner calls “You!”.
  3. Setter calls the set; passer calls “Covering!”.

Score a late or missing call loses the point, even after a good pass.

  • Agree the seam rule first
  • Loud, once — first call stands
  • Face the passer, don't spectate

Next 6v6 where every uncalled touch loses the rally.

Pipe · 15 min · setter, back row, middle

Pipe with a quick decoy

Back-row attack from 6 that holds the middle blocker.

  1. Coach tosses to the setter.
  2. Middle runs a quick as a decoy.
  3. Hitter from ~7 m takes off behind the 3 m line.

Score kill 2 · deep attack 1 · foot fault 0.

  • Start after the middle
  • Take off behind, land in front
  • Same hands for quick and pipe

Next add a live middle blocker.
