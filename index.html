Drill 1

Grow the Team

Small numbers mean players move fast, talk about who sets and play a lot of out-of-system balls. Keep the ball in play and your side gets bigger; one error and everyone goes back to 1 v 1.

  1. 1 v 1, one touch. The coach starts the rally with a free ball.
  2. A team gains a player when it successfully passes the ball over the net. The new player steps in while the ball is on the other side. Play keeps going: no stopping, no new free ball.
  3. Touches = players on your side, up to a maximum of 3.
  4. An error (into the net, out) stops play: both sides go back to 1 v 1 and the coach restarts with a free ball. First side to 6 players wins.

Focus

Communication (who sets?), moving fast and playing out-of-system balls, with the ball kept in play.

Set-up

Players
Two teams of up to 6, starting 1 v 1
Court
Both sides
Line-up
None: players step in as their side grows
Extra
Coach gives the free ball to start, and again after every error

How it runs

  1. Start 1 v 1 from a free ball: one touch, straight back over.
  2. A team gains a player when it successfully passes the ball over the net. The ball stays in play; the coach does not stop it.
  3. Touches = players on the side, max 3.
  4. On an error (into the net, out), stop: both sides go back to 1 v 1 and the coach restarts with a free ball.
  5. First side to reach 6 players wins.
Drill 2

Defence Drill

Not a very intense drill. It is about teaching responsibilities: who covers which ball, and where everyone should be standing when it arrives.

  1. Ready in defence. Six on one court, already switched to their defensive positions. The coach stands on a bench in the middle of the other side.
  2. Coach plays the ball, at random: a down ball, a free ball or a tip.
  3. Cover it and pass to the setter. The front row drops off the net, the setter runs to the target and catches the pass.
  4. Freeze and correct. Stop the players after every ball. Look at where everyone is standing, correct their positions and explain whose ball it was.

Focus

Defensive responsibilities: knowing whose ball it is and where to stand. Low intensity, a teaching drill.

Set-up

Players
6
Court
One side
Line-up
5:1, already switched to defence
Extra
Coach on a bench in the middle of the other side, with balls

How it runs

  1. The coach plays a down ball, free ball or tip, at random.
  2. The team covers the ball and passes it to the setter.
  3. The setter catches it.
  4. Stop after every ball: check where everyone is standing, correct their positions and explain whose ball it was.

Drill 3

Block Cover Drill

The outside hits into the block on purpose, so the ball comes back and the cover has to pick it up. Everyone near the attacker, setter included, learns to be ready to cover every time.

  1. Free ball. Spare players on the other side give a free ball.
  2. Pass to the setter, who sets the outside.
  3. Hit into the block on purpose. The coach holds a board or a big exercise ball as the block. Everyone near the attacker shrinks into cover, setter included.
  4. Cover picks up the rebound. Once the ball is back on the other side, everyone expands to their defensive spots.

Focus

Covering the attacker: everyone near the hitter, setter included, is ready to cover. Shrink when we attack, expand when the ball is on the other side.

Set-up

Players
6, plus spare players
Court
One side
Line-up
5:1, already switched to covering positions
Extra
Coach at the net with a board or big exercise ball as the block; spare players on the other side feed free balls

How it runs

  1. Spare players give a free ball.
  2. Pass to the setter, who sets the outside.
  3. The outside hits into the block on purpose; everyone near her shrinks to cover.
  4. The cover picks up the rebound. Once the ball is back on the other side, everyone expands.
  5. Move the block to pretend a middle or opposite attack.
